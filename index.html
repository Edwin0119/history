Ad Designer & Financial Industry 

【History and Common Ground】

What are the similarities between advertising designers and the financial industry?

Advertising design and the financial industry may seem different, 
one moves people through creativity, the other manages resources through logic.
Yet at their core, both share the same essence: understanding people, delivering value, and building trust.

History of Ad Designer

Ad designers are one of the key professions in modern business and the creative industry.
They use visual elements such as images, typography, colors, and layouts to communicate messages, promote brands, and influence consumer behavior.

The role of the advertising designer did not emerge overnight but gradually developed alongside the growth of commerce, printing technology, and media.



When did advertising design begin?

The origins of Ad design can be traced back to ancient Egypt and Greece,
when merchants carved words or painted images on walls to attract customers , 
these can be considered the earliest forms of advertising.

However, the true profession of the “Ad designer” only emerged after the
Industrial Revolution and the widespread development of printing technology
in the late 19th to early 20th century.

History of Financial Industry

The financial industry is one of the most important pillars of the modern economic system.
Its core functions are to manage capital, allocate resources, and reduce risks.

Although today’s banks, insurance firms, and investment companies appear highly modernized, the origins of financial activities can actually be traced back thousands of years.



When did the financial industry begin?

Financial activities first appeared around 3000 BC in ancient civilizations such as
Babylon, Egypt, and China.

At that time, people began to develop concepts like lending, depositing and interest ,
these formed the earliest foundations of the financial industry.


Examples:

The Code of Hammurabi in ancient Babylon already included
laws regulating interest and debt.

The ancient Egyptians stored surplus grain in “grain banks”
and used it as collateral for loans.

This shows that financial activities already existed in the early
stages of human economic development.               

Common Ground Between Ad Designers and the Financial Industry

Trust as the Core Value
Ad designers build client trust through professionalism and
integrity, ensuring their strategies effectively promote brands.


Human-Centered Thinking
Advertising design focuses on consumers, studying
audience psychology and behavior to craft creative strategies.


Data and Insight-Driven Decisions
Advertising design is not purely about creativity; it is grounded
in market research, consumer data and performance analysis.

Common Ground Between Financial Industry and the  Ad Designers

Trust as the Core Value
Financial advisors must earn investors’ trust through
accurate analysis and effective risk management.


Human-Centered Thinking
The financial industry focuses on clients, understanding
their risk preferences, financial goals, and needs to
provide tailored investment solutions.


Data and Insight-Driven Decisions
The financial sector relies heavily on data analysis,
risk assessment, and financial modeling to guide decision-making.
